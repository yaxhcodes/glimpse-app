https://thenounproject.com/browse/collection-icon/iconly-essential-icons-112852/

Included icons: bookmark, notification, calendar, setting, document, message,
heart, game, location, wallet, star, shield-done, delete, info-circle and logout.
Both Light and Bold SVGs were acquired from Iconbolt's attributed Piqo editions.
Modifications: colors follow Glimpse's theme; Light stroke widths were increased
from 1.5 to 2 on the original 24-unit grid. Bold geometry is unchanged. The
info-circle pair is rotated 180 degrees to read as an information mark.

Interests icons by Epic Icon Designs from Noun Project

https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

Sources:
https://thenounproject.com/icon/interests-6799086/

Modifications: vector redraw of the square, triangle, circle and heart design;
normalized spacing, curves and stroke for Glimpse's 24-unit grid. The outline
and filled states share contours. These are adaptations of the credited designs,
not the original downloadable SVG files. Colors follow the app theme.

Home, Collections, Search, Add link and Add to collection are original Glimpse
vector drawings.
Remaining interface symbols use Phosphor, licensed under MIT by its authors;
the phosphor_flutter package includes its license separately.
